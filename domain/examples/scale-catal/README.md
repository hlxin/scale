# scale-catal examples (synthetic)

Worked examples of the catalysis scale-catal schema
(`domain/graph/scale-catal.yaml`: the science kernel plus the catalysis file, with its one
checklist; schema 0.1.0). Every document follows the checklist (`follows_domains: ["catalysis"]`); what
each catalytic test must report follows from the driving forces it lists. Every value is invented; each document
says so (`data_status: synthetic_example`, `origin: synthetic`).

| file | what it shows |
|---|---|
| `example.json` | A thermocatalytic test (ZnO, CO + 2 H2) with its catalyst sample, feed, reaction, conditions and results as value objects, evidence with locators, REPORTED_IN links and the stewardship of a graph (an invented laboratory and project, public access). |
| `driving-force-examples/*.json` | One document per other driving force or combination: electrocatalysis (static and pulsed, the pulsed one with a nitrate-free blank whose one Faradaic efficiency is hydrogen's), photocatalysis, plasma catalysis, electrothermal, photothermal (static and dynamic), photoelectro- and electrophotocatalysis, each with its controls. Built by `build_examples.py`. |
| `comparison-examples/current-density-comparison.json` | Eight CO2-electroreduction tests on one Ag electrode whose current densities differ only in what a scientific comparison keeps apart: the same potentials vs RHE and vs SHE, geometric and ECSA normalization (the ECSA one naming its denominator with `normalized_by`), a potential converted from Ag/AgCl to RHE with evidence, a basis that contradicts its record, and a normalization or a reference scale not reported. Competency question CQ-06 ranks them in groups (`comparison: scientific`). Built by `build_examples.py`. |
| `product-measurement-examples/product-measurements.json` | Results recorded one by one in `result_values`: a CO2-hydrogenation test with a conversion of 50 %, a methanol yield of 40 % and a selectivity of 80 % (three kinds of share), a second methanol yield by NMR of the condensate (another assay and phase), methane below the GC's detection limit, oxygenates the source does not identify (`unresolved_target`) and a carbon balance of 103 ± 4 % by an Analysis over both assays; a CO2-electroreduction test with Faradaic efficiencies by GC of the headspace (CO at 30 and at 60 min) and NMR of the catholyte (ethanol in both phases), the charge no product accounts for and a charge balance of 97 %; and a repeated electrolysis whose two CO efficiencies nothing tells apart. Competency questions CQ-07 and CQ-08 bind its results. Built by `build_examples.py`. |
| `evidence-examples/kinetic-evidence.json` | What a test is read as showing (`interpretations`) and the evidence the checklist looks for in the records connected to it. CO oxidation rates read as intrinsic kinetics, with transport checks that are numerical (Weisz-Prater and Mears, at 4 % and at 35 % conversion), experimental (finer particles and twice the flow at the same contact time) or only asserted, and a test read as system performance. CO2 methanation under light, with non-thermal effects claimed against dark controls matched at the illuminated surface or only at the bed, and a photothermal effect read from a `ThermalContributionAnalysis`. Plasma-catalytic NH3 synthesis claimed as synergy against the plasma with the bare packing and the catalyst without plasma, with the plasma power apart from the supply's input. Competency questions CQ-09 and CQ-11 run on it. Built by `build_examples.py`. |
| `evidence-examples/dynamic-and-stability.json` | Steam reforming in an electrically heated tube during a ramp from 300 to 800 °C at 10 K/s and a hold (an `Operation` with its segments; the catalyst temperature and the conversion as `TimeSeries`); and O2 evolution on three replicate NiFe oxide electrodes of one `Design` (`REPLICATES`), a constant potential trace and the catalyst after the test by ICP-MS and XPS, beside an electrode read as materially stable from its trace alone, which the checklist reports. CQ-10 runs on it. Built by `build_examples.py`. |
| `state-site-examples/sites-and-assignments.json` | A Cu/ZnO specimen in CO2 hydrogenation to methanol, from an invented paper and an invented reply. Its reduced, working and after-test states each have their own site populations (`HAS_SITE` from the `CatalystState`): 2 % of the exposed Cu atoms are Zn-decorated steps after reduction and 9 % in the working state, by two measurements; 310 µmol/g of exposed Cu after reduction and 240 µmol/g after the test, counted through an Analysis that uses the counting model and its assumptions. One methanol rate gives five turnover frequencies: per exposed Cu atom, per Zn-decorated step (a derived count), at least a value per titrated site (a bounded count), per Cu atom and one that does not say how it was calculated (kept as reported, with a warning); a comparison shows all five side by side, each with its count. A computed state of a slab model has no specimen, and the reply's count for the formulation adds none. Three claims assign roles with named `ABOUT` roles: the ZnOx overlayer, a minority phase, and its Zn-decorated steps active; Cu steps without Zn active (the reply, contradicting the first); bulk ZnO a spectator. Operando Cu K-edge XAS was recorded while the kinetics were measured on the same specimen and matched to the laboratory reactor (`DURING`'s specimen, timing, kinetics and benchmark); operando DRIFTS only overlaps a run, which the checklist reports. A measured spectrum (a Dataset), two fits to it (`spectral_fit`) and the claim citing one stay three records. CQ-14 to CQ-17 run on it. Built by `build_examples.py`. |
| `preparation-examples/impregnation.json` | Pt on gamma-Al2O3 by incipient-wetness impregnation, from an invented paper. Two batches of one nominal 5 wt% formula, made from two lots of H2PtCl6·6H2O (purchased lots as Samples with their lot numbers), each preparation a sequence of steps with its inputs, amounts and conditions; batch 2 impregnated in two portions, a deviation its protocol link states, and reduced at 773 K instead of 573 K. Batch 1's history in order: reduced, passivated, stored 14 days (atmosphere not stated), mounted in air for microscopy, reduced again in the XAS cell, tested. The nominal 5 wt% on the formulation and 3.8 wt% by ICP-OES on the batch; H2 chemisorption and the dispersion it gives; HAADF-STEM size statistics; XRD below its detection limit for batch 1 and a crystallite size for batch 2 (broadening from a LaB6 standard); a Pt L3 EXAFS fit with its model, references and conditions; a BET fit. Two lots of a commercial catalyst, one lot number not stated, each with its own loading and dispersion. CQ-18 to CQ-22 run on it. Built by `build_examples.py`. |
| `preparation-examples/coprecipitation.json` | Cu/ZnO/Al2O3 by coprecipitation, from an invented paper: batch N (carbonate added to the nitrates) and batch R (nitrates added to the carbonate), every other step the same; the solutions as samples with their concentrations; the stirring rate not stated for either, the addition rate not stated for batch R, batch R's storage atmosphere not stated, the XPS spectra not deposited; nominal, bulk (XRF) and surface (XPS) compositions apart. CQ-18 to CQ-20 and CQ-22 run on it. |
| `preparation-examples/high-entropy-alloy.json` | PtPdRhRuIr nanoparticles on carbon by a polyol synthesis, from an invented paper: the nominal equimolar composition on the alloy Material, the bulk by ICP-OES, the surface by XPS as prepared and by near-ambient-pressure XPS under CO oxidation conditions, with the state it establishes (a RuOx minority phase at the surface); a hydrodynamic size of the colloid (DLS), a particle size (HAADF-STEM) and a crystallite size (XRD) as three measurands. CQ-19, CQ-20 and CQ-22 run on it. |
| `computation-examples/computed-energies.json` | O adsorption on close-packed metals, from four invented papers. Each energy is a value of its kind (an electronic adsorption energy, an adsorption free energy, a total energy, an elementary barrier, an apparent activation energy, a degree of rate control) and names the reaction it is the energy change of, whose participants are its reference states. Each computation states its level of theory and charge treatment, which a comparison reads, and its dispersion, spin, solvation, settings, temperature and potential, which it shows; inputs, environment and every structure are checksummed Datasets. The descriptor (O relative to H2O and H2, RPBE, constant charge, on Cu, Pt, Ag and Au) is kept apart from the energy relative to O2, the free energy, an electrochemical free energy at constant charge and two at constant potential (one converted by an analysis) and a machine-learned prediction; a Pd value whose computation states no level of theory and a Ni value whose references are not stated are left out, each with its reason. Two steps' computed barriers with their transition states, an apparent activation energy of CO2 formation from an Arrhenius fit of three measured rates, and degrees of rate control at 450 K and 500 K. A machine-learned model with its checkpoint, its training data and two validations, one on its own training data (which the validator reports). A state computed on the Pt(111) model has no specimen; the model represents a Pt/Al2O3 batch with what that assumes. Every reaction and step balances in atoms and in charge. CQ-23 to CQ-26 run on it. Built by `build_examples.py`. |
| `area-examples/catalysis-areas.json` | Four subdomains of catalysis research that scale-catal 0.7.1 added terms for, from four invented papers. An alcohol dehydrogenase (EC 1.1.1.1) immobilized on silica, a catalyst of family enzyme whose enzyme is a macromolecule; the assay mixture with its substrate, its cofactor (NAD+) and its solvent; the specific activity (U/mg of the enzyme), the activity of the 1 mg charge (U) and per millilitre of the assay mixture (mU/mL), three quantities, and a Michaelis constant for ethanol from a fit. 1-octene hydrogenation by a rhodium phosphine complex with added triphenylphosphine (role ligand) in toluene, with its turnover number. Two transport checks of ethylene hydrogenation over Pd/Al2O3 with Weisz-Prater criteria and Mears criteria for external diffusion (compared by criterion and, for Mears, by the transport judged), internal effectiveness factors and generalized Thiele moduli with their length, and a criterion whose source names none (left out of a comparison). CO2 electrolysis with the electrical energy into the cell per mole and per kilogram of CO, kept apart. Every test states its catalysis mode as its source describes it, and every reaction balances in atoms and in charge. CQ-27 and CQ-28 run on it. Built by `build_examples.py`. |
| `agentic-examples/self-driving-campaign.json` | A campaign in which an agent chooses the next Cu-Zn-Al catalysts for CO2 hydrogenation to methanol, from an invented paper, recorded with what TRACE-AI asks of its workflow: the `Design`'s level of autonomy (A2), human oversight, interventions, the agent's policy and objectives, safety bounds and measures, its stopping criterion and why it stopped, and what was done with failed and excluded runs; each run `PART_OF_DESIGN` with its round and `PROPOSED_BY` the agent with what the operator decided (approved, modified, rejected; one aborted by an alarm). Its Gaussian-process surrogate states its version, architecture, hyperparameters, objective and uncertainty method, is fitted to a located and licensed table, and is validated with folds grouped by composition family (its error with a spread) and with random folds (a lower error stated as one number). The graph says how its records were curated (an extraction agent, autonomy A3) and records the check a person made of them (`checks`: the fields checked, wrong and missed, and a warning acknowledged). `scale trace-ai` reads TRACE-AI on it |
| `transient-examples/transient-kinetics.json` | Tests driven by a program, from an invented paper: an isotope switch at steady state (12CO to 13CO with a Kr tracer) and the residence time and amount of the intermediates of CO2 it gives (`isotopic_transient`); a square-wave CO modulation with operando infrared spectra (one trigger for the valve and the spectrometer) and the phase delays of linear CO, CO2 and carbonate (`modulation_excitation`), which support two claims, linear CO an intermediate and carbonate a spectator; a 63-bit pseudo-random sequence of 5 s bits (exact, its generator given) and one kinetic model fitted to it as plug flow and as a differential reactor, whose two activation energies of one step a comparison shows with their treatments, and a barrier the data do not determine; a chemical loop of four labelled segments. Each `Operation` states its waveform, levels, timing, `program_detail` and `time_origin`; each series its delay |
| `bifunctional-examples/tandem-granule-mixture.json` | The final fixture (provisional concepts, invented values; competency question CQ-12 runs on it) of a bifunctional oxide/zeolite catalyst: each component's `function` (the sub-reaction it performs), `integration_mode` and `integration_scale` of a granule mixture, and a `site_proximity` that is the authors' ordinal description. |
| `source-lifecycle/retracted-source.json` | Two thermocatalytic runs from two invented papers and a retraction notice that retracts the second (a Source of kind `retraction_notice`, a `RETRACTS` link with reason, date and evidence). The validator flags the records that rest on the retracted paper; competency question CQ-05 shows that a query leaves its run out by default and brings it back, labelled, with `include_retracted: true`. |
