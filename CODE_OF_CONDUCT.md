# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this repository, its issues, pull requests and Discussions is expected to be
respectful and constructive: argue about evidence and schemas, never about people; assume good faith;
credit the work of others; and keep personal, unpublished or restricted information out of public threads.
Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not accepted in any form.

To report a concern, write to the owner, Hongliang Xin (GitHub [@hlxin](https://github.com/hlxin)), privately,
at TODO-contact (a contact address the owner adds here before collaborators are invited; until it is set, open an
issue that only asks the owner for a private channel, without describing the concern). Do not report a concern in
an issue. The owner decides what happens, which may be a warning, the removal of content or exclusion from the
repository, and keeps reports confidential. Once reviewers join, the owner and one reviewer not involved in the
concern decide together.
